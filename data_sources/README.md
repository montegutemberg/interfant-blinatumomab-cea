# Data sources

Public documents used for the model inputs:

- SIGTAP: procedure records (SUS price table, competence 03/2026)
- CMED: drug price list (PMVG, 09/10/2026)
- Banco_de_Precos: public procurement prices (BPS 2021 and 2022)
- CONITEC: report no. 725/2022 (blinatumomab)
- Vieira_Martins_2026_tabelas: numbers at risk transcribed from Vieira Martins et al. 2026
- qx_ibge_2024.json: IBGE 2024 complete life table, both sexes
